Questions owners ask us

How do I get an account?

Send us an enquiry. We call you, explain the product and create your library and login credentials for you.

How many seats can I add?

Your library is set up with the number of seats you have. Tell us your seat count in the enquiry and we configure it.

Can half-day students share a seat?

LibManage supports full-day and half-day (morning or evening) plans and counts half-day students separately on the dashboard.

Can my staff use it too?

Yes. You can add employees so they can help run the library day to day.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. There is an Android app, and the web app works in any browser, including Safari on iPhone.
